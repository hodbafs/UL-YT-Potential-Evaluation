# BAFS Group • Talent 2027 Portal Design Direction (design.md)

## 1. Design Philosophy: Executive Gravitas & Aerodynamic Precision
This design speaks with calm, executive confidence. It is the visual equivalent of a private executive boardroom at dawn overlooking the tarmac—steady, grounded, and clear-eyed about the future. 

We strip away all AI gimmicks: no tacky cyber neon glows, no radioactive yellow drop-shadows, no gaming badges, and no theatrical sci-fi portal jargon. Every pixel earns its presence through architectural restraint, refined typography, and purposeful corporate dignity worthy of BAFS Group's leadership.

## 2. Mood & Reference Points
- **Emotional Tone**: Prestigious, Confidential, Forward-looking, Grounded.
- **Visual References**: Linear's dark mode precision, Apple Executive portals, Korn Ferry / Heidrick & Struggles leadership intelligence suites, and Singapore Airlines / Skytrax aviation tier design.
- **Physical Space Metaphor**: An executive aviation suite—deep navy velvet, anodized brushed titanium, champagne gold ambient reflections, and clear glass.

## 3. Typography Direction
- **Primary Fonts**: 
  - English: `Plus Jakarta Sans` / `Inter` (geometric, modern, authoritative)
  - Thai: `Prompt` (contemporary, clean sans-serif with excellent legibility at micro sizes)
- **Hierarchy & Scale**:
  - Brand Header: `1.5rem (24px)`, font-extrabold, tracking-tight
  - Section Title: `1.125rem (18px)`, font-semibold, text-slate-100
  - Body / Labels: `0.75rem - 0.875rem (12px - 14px)`, font-medium, text-slate-300
  - Captions / Footers: `0.6875rem (11px)`, font-normal, text-slate-400
- **Line Heights**: Generous (1.5 - 1.6) for Thai readability; tight (1.2 - 1.25) for headings.

## 4. Color Architecture (Corporate Aviation Navy & Champagne Accent)
- **Background Deep Void**: `#040D1A` (Deep Obsidian Aviation Navy) to `#08172E` (Deep Midnight Blue)
- **Surface Panels**: `rgba(10, 25, 47, 0.72)` with dual hairline borders (`rgba(255,255,255,0.12)` + subtle inner rim light)
- **Primary Brand Tint**: BAFS Aviation Blue (`#005691`), Sky Azure (`#0284c7`), Cyan Muted (`#38bdf8`)
- **Accent Highlight**: Muted Champagne Gold (`#D4AF37` / `#E2B76D`), applied with surgical restraint—never loud yellow.
- **Form Controls**: Deep Slate (`#0B1B33`), with crisp 1px borders (`rgba(255,255,255,0.14)`) and subtle focus ring in corporate azure.

## 5. Anti-Patterns (What We Ban)
- ❌ **No Radioactive Yellow Glows**: Ban `shadow-amber-400/50` or saturated yellow buttons.
- ❌ **No AI Sci-Fi Phrasing**: Ban "เปิดมิติการประเมิน" (sounds like a fantasy warp gate).
- ❌ **No Harsh Raw White Rectangles on Dark Backgrounds**: Frame or integrate corporate logos with harmonious spacing and soft drop shadows.
- ❌ **No Bureaucratic Wording**: Ban dry, lifeless labels like "ระบบประเมินศักยภาพผู้บริหารและบุคลากรกลุ่มเป้าหมาย".
- ❌ **No Saturated Color Orbs**: Ambient light should be faint, diffuse, and cinematic (10-15% opacity), never distracting from reading.

## 6. Signature Design Elements
1. **The Executive Seal & Horizon Tag**: A bespoke, hairline-bordered pill badge with subtle champagne metallic accents designating `TALENT 2027 HORIZON`.
2. **Precision Tactile Input Fields**: Input boxes with slate-cool backdrop and soft inner shadow that feel tangible and secure.
3. **BAFS Royal Azure Action Button**: A crisp, authoritative action button with subtle gradient, clean typography, and purposeful arrow iconography that commands respect without shouting.
